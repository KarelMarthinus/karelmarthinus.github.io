YapFit privacy policy

This page explains how data is used for YapFit’s features. Last updated: 12 October 2026.

Data you provide

YapFit uses the information you enter, such as weight, height, goals, entry dates and related notes, to display your BMI, history and progress. Avoid adding unnecessary sensitive information to free-text notes.

Managing your data

This data is needed for the app’s logging and analysis features. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data on your device may remove any history you have not copied or exported.

Advertising

If your version of YapFit displays adverts, advertising partners may process device identifiers and information about interactions with adverts in accordance with their privacy policies. For more information about AppLovin, see AppLovin’s privacy policy.

Contact
