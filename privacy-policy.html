YapFit

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026
Developer: YapFit Developer

YapFit is an Android app for recording weight, calculating BMI, viewing progress, and managing routines and reminders. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information you enter

YapFit stores your profile name, height, weight entries and dates, optional notes, goals, routines, reminders, and app preferences locally on your device. This information supports the app’s records, calculations, charts, and reminders. An online account is not required.

The current app does not upload your profile contents, weight, BMI, goals, or health notes to a developer-operated server, or send them to Google as advertising request parameters.

Advertising and Google services

YapFit uses Google AdMob to display ads and Google User Messaging Platform to manage privacy choices. The app requests non-personalized ads. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no data is processed.

Google’s advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses, which can be used to estimate general location, device and advertising identifiers, interaction information, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Actual processing depends on device settings, privacy choices, service configuration, and SDK version. Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising SDK data disclosures. Google’s handling and retention of this information are governed by its policies.

Your privacy choices

When required, the app presents a privacy message. When available, the app’s advertising privacy options allow you to revisit your choices. Android settings also provide controls for your advertising identifier. Changing advertising choices does not delete your local weight records.

Exports and backups

You can export records or create backups through the app. CSV exports can be read by anyone who has access to the file. Password-protected backups use encryption; keep your password and backup files secure.

If you select another storage or sharing service, its settings and privacy policy also apply. Deleting app data does not automatically delete exported files or backups outside the app. YapFit’s Android configuration disables automatic app backup and excludes its data from the configured device-transfer rules.

Permissions and local diagnostics

Notification permission supports reminders you enable. The app can reschedule reminders when your device restarts. Advertising services use an internet connection. App preferences and any app-maintained aggregate diagnostic counters are stored locally; YapFit does not upload those counters through an additional developer analytics service.

Retention and deletion

Local records and preferences remain until you delete them. You can delete entries or profiles using the app. Clearing app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app removes its private local data. Delete external exports and backups separately if you no longer want them. The developer cannot recover information stored only on your device.

Security

Local app data is held in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s security mechanisms. Security also depends on your device and how you store or share exported files. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when YapFit’s data practices change. The latest update date appears at the top of this page.
